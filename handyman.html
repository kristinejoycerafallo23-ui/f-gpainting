Free Handyman Service with Full-Property Painting

One team. Painting, minor repairs and preparation — ready for your property transformation.

Why book a separate handyman first?

When you book F&G Decorating for a qualifying full-property painting or larger decorating/refurbishment project, you may receive minor handyman labour free of charge alongside it. It's the benefit of booking a painter and handyman in one: one visit, one team, one invoice.

The idea is simple: you shouldn't need to employ another handyman for several small maintenance jobs before we can paint your property. If it's minor and it's in the way of the decorating, we can often take care of it as part of the project.

For example: a landlord needs an entire flat painted for a new tenancy. Before painting, one door needs adjusting, a toilet seat needs replacing, a damaged socket faceplate needs attention, a small area of plasterboard needs repairing, and the bathroom silicone needs replacing. Rather than hiring a separate handyman for these jobs, we may complete them as part of the qualifying painting project.

Can't see the repair you need?

Just ask. There are too many different maintenance and refurbishment jobs to list everything we can help with. Tell us what you need, or upload a photo, and we'll let you know whether it's something we can take care of.

Terms

How the Free Handyman Service works

  • Free handyman labour is available only with qualifying full-property painting or larger decorating/refurbishment projects.
  • The amount of complimentary handyman work included depends on the size, scope and value of the painting project, and is agreed in your quotation.
  • Materials, replacement parts and specialist items are charged separately, unless specifically included in the quotation.
  • Major electrical, plumbing, gas, structural or specialist works are not included and must be carried out by appropriately qualified trades where required.

In short: booking a small painting job doesn't automatically include hours of free handyman labour — the scope is confirmed with every quote, so there are no surprises.
