About F&G Decorating

We don't simply put paint on walls — we properly prepare and repair the property first, so the finish actually lasts.

Painting, decorating, and the maintenance in between.

F&G Decorating Ltd provides painting, decorating, preparation and minor refurbishment services for homes and businesses across London and the surrounding areas. As an interior painter and exterior painting specialist, we're equally comfortable freshening up a single room or repainting a whole property from the outside in.

As an experienced painter and decorator — and handyman and multitrader — our founder brings a wider set of skills to every project — which is why a booking with us can often cover the small repairs a property needs before it's painted, not just the paint itself.

We're fully insured, and we treat every property — occupied or empty, domestic or commercial — with the same level of care and tidiness.

  • Professional preparation, every time
  • Attention to detail on every surface
  • High-quality, long-lasting finishes
  • Clean and tidy working throughout
  • Reliable, punctual service
  • Residential & commercial experience
  • Minor repairs handled before decorating
Where We Work
