Mould Treatment & Anti-Mould Painting

Treatment and preparation of mould-affected decorating surfaces, finished with a suitable anti-mould paint.

Treating the surface, properly.

We treat and prepare mould-affected decorating surfaces: removing affected or loose coatings, applying appropriate treatment or primer where required, and finishing with a suitable anti-mould paint.

Please note: if mould is caused by an underlying leak, penetrating damp, a ventilation problem or a structural issue, that underlying cause may need to be corrected first. We do not advertise this as guaranteed permanent mould removal — our work addresses the decorating surface, not the root structural or moisture issue.

What's included

Who It's For

Bathrooms, kitchens and poorly ventilated rooms.

Mould on decorating surfaces is common in bathrooms, kitchens and rooms with limited ventilation. We'll assess the affected area when you request a quote and let you know honestly what we can do — and flag if an underlying cause looks like it needs attention first.
