Privacy Policy

How F&G Decorating Ltd collects, uses and protects your personal information.

Last updated: October 2026

F&G Decorating Ltd ("we", "us") provides painting, decorating and property maintenance services across London and the surrounding areas. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, how we use it, and your rights under UK data protection law (UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018).

Who we are

F&G Decorating Ltd is the data controller for the information collected through this website. You can contact us about privacy at fgdecoratingltd@gmail.com or on 07351 052123.

What we collect

  • Details you give us in the quote form, enquiry popup or contact page: name, phone number, email address, property postcode and a description of the work.
  • Photos or videos of your property that you choose to upload or send via WhatsApp so we can prepare an accurate quote.
  • Messages you send us by email, phone or WhatsApp.
  • Basic technical information (such as browser type and pages visited) if you accept analytics cookies — see our Cookie Policy.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare a quotation.
  • To arrange surveys, visits and the work itself if you go ahead.
  • To keep records of quotes and jobs, and to meet our legal and accounting obligations.

Our legal bases are your request to receive a quote or service from us (steps before entering a contract), our legitimate interest in running and improving our business, and legal obligation where records must be kept.

Photos and videos

Property photos and videos are used only to prepare and discuss your quotation and the job. We do not publish photos of your property, or any images showing you or your home, without asking for your permission first.

Sharing your information

We never sell your details or share them with third parties for marketing. We only share information with service providers who help us run the business (for example email, messaging or website hosting providers), and only where needed to provide those services, or where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and quote details for as long as needed to deal with your request and, where a job goes ahead, for the period needed for accounting and legal purposes as required. Enquiries that do not lead to a job are not kept longer than necessary.

Your rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, or move it to another provider. To make a request, contact us at fgdecoratingltd@gmail.com. We will reply within one month. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data you can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
