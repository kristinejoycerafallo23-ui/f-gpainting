Wallpapering

Wallpaper hanging and the careful preparation it depends on, for a clean, professional finish.

Preparation is most of the job.

Good wallpapering stands or falls on what happens before the paper goes up. We strip old paper, line and level walls where needed, and make sure surfaces are properly prepared before hanging — so patterns line up and the finish stays flat and secure.

Whether it's a single feature wall or a full room, we treat wallpapering with the same attention to preparation and detail as our painting work.
