Cookie Policy

How this website uses cookies and similar technologies.

Last updated: October 2026

This page explains how F&G Decorating Ltd uses cookies and similar technologies on this website.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser. They help websites work, remember your choices and understand how a site is used.

How we use them

  • Essential: this site uses your browser's session storage to remember that you have already seen the enquiry popup, so it does not keep reappearing during your visit. This does not identify you.
  • Embedded content: if you follow a link to our TikTok, Instagram, Facebook or WhatsApp, those services set their own cookies under their own policies. We do not control them.
  • We do not use advertising cookies, and we do not sell data collected through cookies.

Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking essential storage will not stop the site working, but the enquiry popup may appear more than once.

Contact

Questions about cookies or privacy: fgdecoratingltd@gmail.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
